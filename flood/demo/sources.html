  • OpenFEMA data sets: NFIP claims (v3), NFIP policies (v3), NFIP residential penetration rates, disaster declarations summaries (v2)

  • Census ACS summary files: 2019–2023 5-year tables B19013, B25077 and B25001
  • BLS Consumer Price Index: CPI-U, US city average
  • NOAA Storm Events Database: 2015–2024 details
